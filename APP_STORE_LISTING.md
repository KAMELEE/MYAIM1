```
MY AIM is your platform to discover academies, coaches, and training programs in one place — and reach your goals with clear steps.

Whether you are looking for a sports academy, an educational course, a self-development program, or a new tech skill, MY AIM gives you trusted options near you, transparent prices, and an easy booking experience.

• Discover what's near you
Browse academies and services by your location on a map or a list, and filter by category, price, and rating.

• Compare with confidence
Clear details for every service: description, price, ratings, and real reviews — no surprises.

• Chat directly
Message the academy by text or voice note before booking, and ask about schedules and details.

• Book easily and safely
Send a booking request and pay by bank transfer or QR code; the academy then confirms your booking and arranges the schedule with you.

• For academies and coaches
A complete dashboard to run your academy: add courses, publish posts, manage and approve booking requests, and track your performance.

Sports academies · Educational academies · Self-development · Tech services

Start today — set your goal, and we'll walk the path with you.
```
